[ PRODUCTION & PROCESSING TIME ]

To ensure the highest quality, all Babaika pieces are created to order. The production process begins only once your payment is fully confirmed. Please allow approximately two weeks for the creation of your item. As soon as your piece is completed and packed, it will be dispatched, and you will receive a shipping confirmation with your tracking information. Transit times will apply from the date of shipment, not the date of purchase.

[ SALES POLICY ]

Because every piece is made to order upon payment, all sales are final. We cannot offer refunds or exchanges for change of mind or incorrect sizing. Since our wraps are designed as one-size, please contact us if you have any specific sizing questions, or feel free to include your measurements/specifications in the notes at checkout. If you require a resize after receiving your piece, please contact us within 7 days; resizing services are subject to a studio fee and return shipping costs.

[ HANDMADE VARIATIONS ]

Each Babaika object is individually crafted by hand. Due to the nature of our production process, slight variations in texture, metal finish, or textile drape are to be expected and celebrated. These nuances ensure your piece is entirely unique and are not considered defects.

[ SHIPPING ADDRESS ]

Please ensure your shipping address is entered correctly at checkout. We are not responsible for lost packages or returned shipments due to incorrect or incomplete addresses provided by the buyer. If a package is returned to our studio for this reason, the buyer is responsible for the cost of re-shipping.

[ CUSTOMS & DUTIES ]

If a package is returned to us because the recipient refused to pay local customs duties or taxes, or failed to claim the package from their local courier, any return shipping fees incurred by our studio will be deducted from a potential store credit.
